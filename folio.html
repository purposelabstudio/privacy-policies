Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Folio ("the App") is a quiet digital notebook for closing the day, developed by PurposeLab Studio. We believe your words are yours alone. This policy explains what data we collect (very little), how we handle it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Folio does not collect any personal data. We do not require accounts, email addresses, phone numbers, names, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).

The App may collect anonymous, aggregated crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics) to improve stability. This data cannot identify individual users. No analytics tracking is performed on your journal content.

Data Storage

All your journal entries, daily marks, monthly themes, habits, and preferences are stored locally on your device in an SQLite database. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

If you use the optional Google Drive backup feature, your data is transferred directly between your device and your personal Google Drive account. PurposeLab does not have access to your Google Drive or your backed-up data.

Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

Post Notifications (Android 13+): To send a gentle evening reminder to write your daily page. You can deny or disable this at any time in Settings.

Biometric / Device Credentials: Only requested if you enable App Lock. Used solely to authenticate you locally — no biometric data is collected or stored by the App.

Internet Access: Required only for optional Google Drive backup and in-app purchases. The App works fully offline.

Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing: Used for subscriptions or one-time purchases. Google processes payment information directly — Folio never sees or stores your payment details.

Firebase Crashlytics: Anonymous crash reports to help us fix bugs. No PII is collected. Advertising ID collection is disabled.

Google Drive API: Used only if you opt into backup. We request the minimum scope needed to read/write a single app-specific backup file in your Drive.

Your Journal Content

We want to be explicit: we never read, access, analyze, or transmit your journal entries. Your writing stays on your device (or in your personal Google Drive if you choose to back up). There is no server-side processing of your content.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties. We do not serve advertisements. We do not use ad tracking or retargeting.

Children's Privacy

Folio is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the App is compliant with COPPA.

Your Rights

You can delete all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app. Since we don't collect personal data on our servers, there is nothing to request deletion of on our end.

If you have used Google Drive backup, you can delete the backup file directly from your Google Drive at any time.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: hello@purposelab.studio

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
