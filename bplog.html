Privacy Policy

BP Log — Blood Pressure Tracker

Last updated: May 5, 2026

BP Log does NOT collect any personal data. The app operates entirely offline on your device. No data is ever transmitted to any server.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores the following data in a local database on your device only:

  • Blood pressure readings (systolic and diastolic values)
  • Pulse rate (optional)
  • Notes you add to readings
  • Timestamps of readings
  • App preferences (reminder settings, display preferences)

Data NOT Collected

  • No personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers
  • No usage analytics or crash reports
  • No health data is transmitted to any server

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your Android device using an application-private database. The data is sandboxed within the app's private storage and cannot be accessed by other applications.

Data Sharing

BP Log does NOT share your data with any third parties. There are no advertising SDKs, analytics services, cloud synchronization, server-side storage, or third-party data processors.

Data Export

You may choose to export your data as PDF or CSV files using the app's export feature. Exported files are saved to your device and shared only through your explicit action (e.g., email, messaging).

Data Deletion

You can delete individual readings within the app at any time. To delete all app data, uninstall the app or clear the app's data through Android Settings.

Internet Permission

BP Log does NOT require internet access. The app does not request the INTERNET permission in its Android manifest.

Children's Privacy

BP Log does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is rated "Everyone" and contains no age-restricted content.

Medical Disclaimer

BP Log is for recording and tracking purposes only. The app does not measure blood pressure and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical decisions.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
